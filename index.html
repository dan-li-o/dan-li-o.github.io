About

Thinking like a writer, prototyping like an engineer.

I move between essays, facilitation, and code. Sometimes that looks like building a bespoke tool for a founder, other times it means coaching a team through better story architecture. Either way, the goal is the same: leave things clearer than we found them.

When a problem feels messy, I map it, label the tensions, and design light-touch systems that invite people to act. Clarity is a craft, not a constraint.
